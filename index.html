Importron

Your labs. In Health.

A native iPhone utility for reviewing the original Lab Results of Record PDF from your Function export and adding a personal transcription to Apple Health. In development.

Support

Get help on GitHub

Support issues are public. Describe the problem without attaching lab PDFs, patient details or medical information. Include only your app version and iOS version if relevant.

Which PDFs work?

Choose the original text-based Health Gorilla Lab Results of Record export. Scans and other layouts are not supported. Review the transcription before adding it. An ambiguous result stops the import.

Where are my results?

After tapping Add my results to Health, finish the confirmation in Health. Back in Importron, the optional check can compare the saved lab entries with your PDF. When every result matches, a dedicated screen says Verified in Apple Health and All N results match your PDF. This confirms that the app read the saved records back from Apple Health. Already-readable records verify without another permission request; Apple may require clinical-record consent for first access or newly added records. If a check is unsuccessful, inspect Health before adding anything else. Attempts are protected before the handoff, even when verification is skipped. Reopening an attempted or overlapping report blocks another import. If you imported with an earlier version or lost local history, choose Verify existing results before adding. Confirmed imports cannot be reset. Only reset an unconfirmed attempt if you canceled in Health without saving; the app requires explicit confirmation.

Purchases

Free download includes PDF review. Imports require an active yearly subscription, planned at US $9.99/year, with local pricing displayed before purchase. Use Restore purchases for an existing subscription. Manage or cancel in your Apple Account settings. Records already added to Health remain there after cancellation.
