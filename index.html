Importron

Your labs. In Health.

A native iPhone utility for reviewing the original Lab Results of Record PDF from your Function export and adding a personal transcription to Apple Health. In development.

Support

Get help on GitHub

Support issues are public. Describe the problem without attaching lab PDFs, patient details or medical information. Include only your app version and iOS version if relevant.

Which PDFs work?

Choose the original text-based Health Gorilla Lab Results of Record export. Scans and other layouts are not supported. Review the transcription before adding it. An ambiguous result stops the import.

Where are my results?

Choose a PDF and review the patient and results. The Apple Health section in that same report shows its status. Add to Apple Health opens Apple's confirmation sheet; finish adding there, then return to Importron.

Importron automatically checks records you have already allowed when you open a report or return from Health. A full match shows In Apple Health and All N results match this PDF. There is no second success screen. The navigation-bar close button only closes the report.

If the status is Import not confirmed, choose Check Apple Health. The explanation beside the action describes optional Lab Results read access. Apple may call this sharing records with Importron and may need consent for newly added records. No permission screen opens from an automatic check. An unavailable or partial read is never treated as success or proof that records are absent.

Reopening the same or an overlapping report does not send another copy or require another purchase. Import history explains the local matching. Previously confirmed results cannot be reset. If you canceled in Health without saving, use Canceled without saving? and confirm that exact attempt. Only then can the same free or purchased import be retried.

Purchases

Your first complete PDF import is free once. After that, each PDF is a separate US $1.00 purchase, with local pricing shown before checkout. There is no subscription or automatic renewal. Reviewing PDFs and verifying existing records are always free. If you cancel without saving in Health, reset that canceled attempt to keep the same import available for retry. Recover unfinished purchase can complete an interrupted purchase. Used imports are not restored from purchase history.

Try a sample

Download the synthetic sample PDF. It contains 119 invented results and no real patient data. A second synthetic PDF has a different collection date, for testing the paid import after the free one. For testing and App Review only; do not add these invented records to your personal Health history.
