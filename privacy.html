Privacy policy · October 23, 2026

Your PDF and results

Importron reads the PDF you choose on your iPhone. It does not upload your PDF, patient details or lab results to an Importron server, an AI service or an analytics service. There is no Importron account, advertising or tracking.

Your report stays in memory while you review it. Closing the report clears that in-memory copy. Your original PDF stays wherever you saved it.

Apple Health

You choose whether to add the transcribed results in Health. Health stores a personal transcription by Importron. The original laboratory report remains authoritative.

When you open or reopen a PDF, return from Health, or refresh the report, Importron checks already-accessible Lab Results without requesting new permission. A complete match confirms the saved report; partial or unavailable records never prove success or absence. An explicit Check Apple Health action can request optional Lab Results read access after an explanation in the report. Apple uses a separate permission screen for clinical Health Records, may call this sharing with Importron, and may require renewed consent for newly added records. Closing a report does not grant access or confirm saving.

Importron compares each selected PDF result with accessible Lab Results, including values, units, dates, reference context, flags and notes. Patient-bound import identifiers establish exact saved matches. Same-assay and collection-time candidates without proven identity conservatively block an uncertain repeat. Saved, conflicting and unconfirmed results are skipped; only eligible new results are sent for your confirmation. The comparison occurs on your iPhone. Results read from Health are not retained by Importron or sent to us.

Records you add to Health remain there after you close Importron or delete Importron. Apple controls Health storage and any syncing you enable. Manage Health permissions and delete Health records in the Health app.

Import history

Before opening Health, Importron saves hashes of the report and its patient/test/collection identifiers, together with whether the import was confirmed and an optional opaque allowance reservation ID. The actual submitted subset is retained separately from whole-report confirmation. Exact saved matches and observed conflicting or uncertain events also retain hash protection. This prevents recognized repeats even if you skip verification, close the app or later lose read access; independent new rows in an overlapping PDF remain eligible. The history contains no copy of the PDF, patient fields or lab values. It has device file protection and is excluded from backups. Confirmed imports cannot be reset in the app. An unconfirmed attempt can be reset only after you explicitly confirm you canceled in Health without saving. Deleting the app removes this local protection but does not delete Health records. Accessible clinical records can also block a repeat; unavailable records never prove an import is absent.

Purchases

Apple handles one-time payments through StoreKit. We do not receive payment-card details. The lifetime free-use marker, verified transaction IDs and opaque reservation IDs are stored in this iPhone’s secure Keychain, separate from lab data. They do not reset each year. An encrypted device backup can transfer these items to another iPhone. There is no Importron account or server balance. Finished transaction history is checked for prior purchase evidence and refunds; it never grants used imports again. If you cancel without saving in Health, explicit recovery makes the same import available again. Deleting the app does not automatically clear Keychain purchase usage.

External websites

Links open in your browser. Website providers may receive ordinary connection information such as an IP address. This website has no advertising, analytics, cookies or forms. This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may maintain normal connection logs; see GitHub’s privacy statement.
