Privacy policy · October 22, 2026

Your PDF and results

Importron reads the PDF you choose on your iPhone. It does not upload your PDF, patient details or lab results to an Importron server, an AI service or an analytics service. There is no Importron account, advertising or tracking.

Your report stays in memory while you review it. Closing the report clears that in-memory copy. Your original PDF stays wherever you saved it.

Apple Health

You choose whether to add the transcribed results in Health. Health stores a personal transcription by Importron. The original laboratory report remains authoritative.

The optional confirmation check requests permission to read Lab Results. It compares entries bearing Importron’s patient-bound import identifiers with your chosen PDF, including results, units, dates, reference context, flags and notes. The comparison occurs on your device. Results read from Health are not retained by Importron or sent to us.

Records you add to Health remain there after you close Importron, delete Importron or cancel a subscription. Apple controls Health storage and any syncing you enable. Manage Health permissions and delete Health records in the Health app.

Import history

After a confirmed import, Importron saves a fingerprint of the report to help prevent duplicates. The history contains no copy of the PDF, patient details or lab values. It has device file protection and is excluded from backups. Clear it in About → Clear import history, or remove the app. Clearing this history does not delete Health records.

Subscriptions

Apple handles payments and subscription transactions. Importron checks your purchase entitlement through StoreKit. We do not receive payment-card details. Cancel or manage your subscription in your Apple Account settings.

External websites

Links open in your browser. Website providers may receive ordinary connection information such as an IP address. This website has no advertising, analytics, cookies or forms. This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may maintain normal connection logs; see GitHub’s privacy statement.

Contact

If you need help, open a support issue on GitHub. Issues are public. Do not attach lab PDFs, patient details or other medical information. We use the information you choose to provide to respond to your request. GitHub stores your post under its own policies. You can edit your post or request removal from the repository owner.
